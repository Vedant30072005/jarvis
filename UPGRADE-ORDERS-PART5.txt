SECTION 1 — ORIENTATION: THE FOUR BLIND SPOTS OF PARTS I–IV
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B1. THE ADVERSARY. Every part so far treats news as honest-but-noisy. It is
    neither. News is a battlefield where promoters, PR firms and pump
    operators actively manufacture the exact statistical footprints my
    engine calls "signal". An engine that trusts repetition is an engine
    that can be PLAYED.
B2. ABSENCE. The engine only reads what arrives. Silence — coverage
    collapsing, expected events not materialising — carries information
    that presence does not.
B3. THE NULL HYPOTHESIS. Nothing yet checks whether the engine's output is
    distinguishable from noise. A tool this pretty deserves the dignity of
    being falsifiable.
B4. THE ENDGAME. The tool measures the market and the user — but never
    renders the final verdict: is any of this active effort beating the
    boring alternative? The tool must be able to conclude "no" and say so.

SECTION 2 — THE ADVERSARY (1701 + one critical amendment)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIRST, A FLAW IN MY OWN FLAGSHIP — CRITICAL AMENDMENT TO ORD-1001:
  The Anomaly Sonar as specified is a PUMP-AND-DUMP AMPLIFIER. A coordinated
  promotion of an obscure smallcap produces precisely the sonar's trigger
  profile: sudden term frequency, multiple (low-grade) "sources", zero prior
  baseline. Without a guard, the shiniest feature in this program would
  faithfully surface manipulation as opportunity — with a glow effect.
  MANDATORY GUARD: an anomaly whose term maps to a single COMPANY (rather
  than a sector/theme/commodity/policy) AND that company is not in the
  curated large/midcap universe (ORD-303) renders as "UNVERIFIED SURGE —
  promotion-shaped profile" in amber, is EXCLUDED from idea generation
  entirely (hard rule), and links to the astroturf tooltip below. Theme- and
  policy-level anomalies keep full status. Implement WITH ORD-1001, not after.

ORD-1701  ASTROTURF / HYPE FILTER. [P1 — protects everything downstream]
  Score every signal on manufacture-likelihood; heuristics, all cheap:
   a) HEDGE DENSITY: per-100-words count of "reportedly, sources say, may,
      could, mulls, considering, in talks, likely to, planning to, expected
      to". High hedge = low commitment journalism.
   b) SUPERLATIVE DENSITY: "multibagger, massive, huge, golden opportunity,
      game-changer, guaranteed, unstoppable, next <famous company>". Any hit
      is a red flag — real filings never talk like this.
   c) UNNAMED SOURCING: "sources/people familiar" with no named entity.
   d) OUTLET TIER: source not on the configured wire list = untiered.
   e) COORDINATION: ≥3 near-identical bullish pieces on one small entity
      within 2h from untiered outlets (extends ORD-1603 syndication) →
      "COORDINATED" flag.
   f) PRICE-LED-NEWS (quotes live): instrument already +15% in 5 sessions
      BEFORE the coverage burst → "the market knew first" note.
  hypeScore = weighted sum; above threshold → amber HYPE badge on the card,
  impact multiplied by 0.5, excluded from flows and ideas. The badge tooltip
  is headed by the only question that matters:
      "WHO BENEFITS FROM YOU BELIEVING THIS?"
  False positives are acceptable; this filter guards the till. Log filtered
  items to a collapsible "quarantine" list (Intel view footer) so the human
  can audit the filter's judgement — filters must be auditable (Art. 5/6).

DOCTRINE — THE INFORMATION FOOD CHAIN (write into ARCHITECTURE.md):
  Information reaches layers in order: insiders → brokers/channel checks →
  institutional research → financial media → aggregators → retail
  dashboards. JARVIS sits at layer 5-6. Three things survive to this layer
  with value intact: LAG (multi-quarter structural themes), SYNTHESIS
  (cross-domain chains — the causal engine's job), NEGLECT (things too
  small or boring for institutional coverage). Immediacy does NOT survive.
  Any future feature whose pitch is "react faster" is out of scope by
  doctrine, permanently. (This hardens Part III E1-E3 into a filter for
  feature proposals.)

ORD-1709  THE SEAT AT THE TABLE (pond-size honesty). [P2, small]
  Tag every watch name in JDATA with a cap band (large/mid/small — static,
  maintained yearly). Idea cards show a POND note: large-cap theme → "your
  only edge here is patience; institutions have the rest"; small-cap →
  "neglect edge possible; liquidity risk real — exits take days, not
  clicks". Retail's structural advantages (no size impact, no career risk,
  no mandate, may hold 100% cash) and disadvantages (latency, access,
  execution) go into the Playbooks intro as a one-screen doctrine page.

SECTION 3 — ABSENCE AS SIGNAL (1702–1703)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ORD-1702  THE QUIET BOARD. [P2 — needs ≥30d archive]
  Inverse of the sonar: sector whose trailing-7d signal rate falls below 25%
  of its own 30-day baseline (baseline ≥ 2 signals/day required) enters the
  QUIET list. Small dim panel next to the Threat Board: "Gone quiet:
  Metals (−82% coverage)". Framing is strictly contrarian-prompt, not
  signal: "Coverage collapse is usually boredom. Occasionally it is the
  bottom of a cycle. Either way, nobody is looking — which is when looking
  is cheapest." Style: deliberately muted — low-opacity glass, no glow. The
  one panel in the app that is VISUALLY quiet, by design.

ORD-1703  EXPECTED-BUT-MISSING EVENTS. [P3, small]
  JDATA.EVENTS entries may declare expectTag + expectWindow (e.g. MPC →
  "Central Banks" within 24h). If the window passes with zero matching
  signals, the next brief notes it once: "The MPC window passed without
  wire coverage — either the sensors missed it or the outcome was a
  non-event. Worth thirty seconds of manual checking." Dual purpose:
  genuine absence-signal + free sensor diagnostics (PM1).

SECTION 4 — THE NULL MODEL: IS THE ENGINE BETTER THAN NOISE? (1704)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ORD-1704  NULL-MODEL HARNESS. [P1, cheap, scientifically honest]
  (a) SCRAMBLE TEST (test.html): take the current corpus; shuffle words
      across titles (preserving lengths) → run the full engine → compare
      against the real run: cluster count, conviction spread, top-idea
      conviction. If scrambled output resembles real output, conviction
      thresholds are decorating noise — raise the bar until the real corpus
      clearly separates. Print both distributions; rerun after every engine
      change; the golden-corpus diff (ORD-1305) shows drift.
  (b) CONTROL ARM (in the accountability report, ORD-1003): alongside real
      flagged patterns, the report silently tracks a RANDOM-SECTOR pick
      made the same day (seeded from the date, so it's reproducible). The
      report card must show: flagged patterns' 30d proxy performance vs
      the random control's. If the engine does not beat its own placebo,
      the report says so in plain text. No dashboard has ever been this
      honest with its owner; this one will be.

SECTION 5 — THE SHADOW BENCHMARK & THE VERDICT ENGINE (1705–1706)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ORD-1705  SHADOW BENCHMARK (the counterfactual book). [P1 logic, P2 data]
  Mirror every real equity/MF BUY of ₹X on date D into a shadow position of
  X / NIFTY_close(D) index units; SELLs remove units proportionally. Shadow
  value = units × current index level. Cash stays cash on both sides;
  dividends ignored on both sides (state this). Index closes via the relay
  (Yahoo ^NSEI daily series, cached) with a manual monthly-close table as
  fallback (settings → paste/edit). Persist shadow lots alongside txns.
  My Money gains one honest line under net worth:
     "vs SHADOW (all-index): you are +₹18,400 (+2.1pp) / −₹9,200 (−1.0pp)"
  INVARIANT (ORD-1604 list grows): shadow units only change on txn events.

ORD-1706  THE VERDICT ENGINE (self-obsolescence protocol). [P1 — the
  endgame feature; Art. 1 demands it exists]
  Yearly (or on demand once ≥ 12 months and ≥ 10 txns exist), synthesize:
  book XIRR vs shadow XIRR · journal reason-tag performance (ORD-1103) ·
  calibration Brier (ORD-1606) · accountability vs control arm (ORD-1704b)
  · hours proxy (app sessions × est. minutes). Render ONE of three verdicts,
  template-written with his real numbers:
   V1 EDGE EVIDENT: "Active decisions added +Npp over the shadow book.
      Whatever you're doing in the journal's winning tags — keep doing it,
      size it modestly up, change nothing else."
   V2 INCONCLUSIVE: "N months is not evidence. Continue, but cap active
      bets at 25% of the book until the data says otherwise."
   V3 NO EDGE: "The index beat your active decisions by Npp this year, at a
      cost of roughly M hours. The strongest move available to you is:
      automate the SIP, keep a 10% sandbox for the itch, and close this
      terminal more often. I will still be here for the ledger."
  V3 must be reachable and renderable — a tool that can only flatter is a
  toy. This is the deepest loyalty JARVIS can offer: recommending less of
  himself when the numbers say so. The verdict goes in the report card AND
  the yearly maintenance ritual (ORD-1512).

SECTION 6 — TAX AWARENESS, INDIA (1707) — real money is after-tax money
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ORD-1707  TAX MODULE. [P1 — high real-world value, pure arithmetic]
  Static config JDATA.TAX_IN keyed by financial year: {fy, stcgRate,
  ltcgRate, ltcgExemptionLimit, equityLtcgHoldingDays, advanceTaxDates[]}.
  IMPLEMENTATION RULE: the implementing model must NOT trust its memory for
  current rates — leave placeholder values with a "CONFIRM WITH USER"
  comment; the human fills them from current rules, and the yearly
  maintenance protocol (ORD-1512) re-confirms each April.
  Features (all derived from the FIFO ledger, which already knows every
  lot's holding period):
   a) Realized gains YTD split STCG/LTCG, with estimated tax at configured
      rates.
   b) LTCG exemption utilisation bar ("₹X of the exemption limit used").
   c) HARVEST WINDOW (Jan–Mar): unrealized LTCG within exemption headroom
      and unrealized losses eligible to offset — listed as arithmetic
      ("selling lot L would realise ₹Y LTCG against ₹Z remaining
      exemption"), with the standing line: "Numbers, not advice — confirm
      with a CA before acting." (G8 applies with full force here.)
   d) Advance-tax dates join the events calendar and the .ics export.
  Jarvis intents: "tax summary", "harvest check". My Money gets a slim TAX
  strip, visible only after the first SELL/DIV transaction exists.

SECTION 7 — THE BUILDER'S TRAP (amendment to ORD-1615)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tinkering is the most pleasant form of procrastination, and this five-part
program is itself a temptation. Extend the dust report (ORD-1615):
  Track two counters: building-sessions (any session that changed code) and
  decisions-logged (journal entries + predictions + transactions). Once per
  quarter, if building-sessions > 5 × decisions-logged, JARVIS says — once,
  dryly, dismissibly: "This quarter: 14 building sessions, 2 investing
  decisions. The tool is becoming the hobby, Sir. That is allowed — but
  let us call it what it is." (Art. 3: once, then silence.)
The same trap governs THIS document series — see Section 10.
