SECTION 1 — DOCTRINE: WHAT EDGE CAN THIS TOOL ACTUALLY GIVE?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Be honest about the causal chain. News is priced into large caps within
minutes by professionals with faster feeds and better models. A retail user
with a news dashboard CANNOT win on speed — and the tool must not pretend
otherwise. The three edges that ARE realistically available:

E1. TIME-HORIZON ARBITRAGE. Institutions must perform quarterly; a person
    can hold a 3-year capex theme through drawdowns. Multi-quarter themes
    (gov capex cycles, PLI buildouts, energy transition) reward patience,
    not reaction time. → The tool's center of gravity is THREADS and
    PATTERN DELTAS over weeks (ORD-1002, ORD-203), not the live ticker.
    The ticker is theater; the archive is the edge.

E2. ATTENTION ARBITRAGE (SECOND ORDER). The market reprices the obvious
    beneficiary in minutes (HAL on a fighter deal). It takes weeks to fully
    reprice tier-2 and tier-3 of the value chain (forgings, connectors,
    explosives, castings, testing houses). Mapping "who ELSE wins, later"
    is encodable as static data and is the highest-alpha feature in this
    entire program. → ORD-1501.

E3. DISCIPLINE EDGE. Most retail losses come from process failure —
    oversizing, chasing, no exit plan — not from lacking information. The
    checklist gate, journal, sizing calculator and behavioral guardrails
    (Part II sections E/F) are not accessories; they ARE the product.

DESIGN CONSEQUENCES (bind these as rules):
D1. THE APP NEVER HAS A BUY BUTTON. It records decisions; it never executes
    or urges them. No "act now", no countdown urgency on ideas, ever.
D2. Anti-speed principle: nothing auto-refreshes on an interval (R7 stands).
    A calm tool that is right beats a twitchy tool that is fast.
D3. Depth features (threads, value chains, deltas, accountability) always
    outrank speed/spectacle features when sprints must be cut.

SECTION 2 — PRE-MORTEM: "IT'S 2027 AND VEDANT STOPPED OPENING THE APP. WHY?"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Five most likely deaths, and the mitigation each one demands:

PM1. ROT — feeds silently died, relay not running, sim data feels stale,
     the app feels like an abandoned aquarium. → Staleness must be VISIBLE
     and graceful (ORD-1507), coverage panel (ORD-904), zero-maintenance
     defaults (sim mode always works), yearly maintenance ritual (ORD-1512).
PM2. DISTRUST — he once caught a wrong number (double-counted crore, stale
     price shown as live) and never believed the dashboard again. Trust is
     lost in one incident. → Integrity orders are P0 (ORD-901), every number
     answers "says who / as of when" (ORD-306, 1507), accountability report
     shows misses too (ORD-1003).
PM3. OVERWHELM — the morning protocol crept from 1 minute to 10; panels
     multiplied; opening the app feels like homework. → IA restraint is law
     (ORD-1404), progressive disclosure, "no new sidebar items", the brief
     must fit one screen.
PM4. GUILT MIRROR — a drawdown made the app an anxiety object; avoidance
     followed. → Persona rules on losses (Section 7), drawdown framing in
     runway months (ORD-1203), "do nothing" as a valid verdict (ORD-1204),
     never red-flash the net worth number.
PM5. NOVELTY DECAY — the animations thrilled for a week, then annoyed.
     → One-key calm: performance/fx modes already exist; add auto-suggest
     after 10 sessions ("Shall I dial the theatrics down 20%, Sir?") — a
     single polite offer, never repeated after refusal (fold into ORD-703).

SECTION 4 — EXACT MATH (implement precisely; each formula gets a test)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
M1. NOVELTY z (ORD-1001): for term t with today-count c and daily history
    over D days (require D ≥ 7 else "warming up"): winsorize history at its
    95th percentile, μ = mean, σ = sd. z = (c − μ) / max(σ, √μ, 1).
    Anomaly ⇔ z ≥ 3 AND c ≥ 3 AND distinct sources ≥ 2. If a bigram and its
    unigrams all fire, keep only the bigram. Cap displayed anomalies at 6.
M2. SIMILARITY: 3-word shingles of lowercased title; Jaccard J = |A∩B|/|A∪B|.
    J ≥ 0.55 → duplicates (merge into Group). 0.35 ≤ J < 0.55 → thread
    candidates. Also duplicate if same sector AND same company AND amounts
    within ±10% within 72h.
M3. IMPACT PERCENTILE (ORD-903): impactPct = round(100·rank/(n+1)), ties
    averaged, clamped 1..99. Keep impactRaw for archive comparability.
M4. RECENCY DECAY: w(h) = exp(−h·ln2/48) — 48h half-life; items older than
    7 days leave live momentum entirely (they persist in rollups).
M5. CONVICTION v2: conv = clamp(0.40·clusterScore
      + 0.20·min(sources/6,1)·100
      + 0.20·min(log10(max(flowsCr,1))/6,1)·100
      + 0.20·(((bull−bear)/members)+1)/2·100, 20, 96)
    Store the four components on the Idea for the lineage tooltip.
M6. XIRR: Newton-Raphson from r=0.10, ≤60 iterations, tolerance 1e-7; on
    divergence fall back to bisection on [−0.9999, 10]. Preconditions: ≥2
    flows, both signs present, span ≥ 30 days — else display "—". Buys are
    negative flows, sells/dividends positive, terminal book value positive.
M7. FIFO LOTS: per (name|symbol) a lot queue. SELL consumes oldest lots;
    qty greater than held → reject with a clear toast (no negative lots).
    Realized P&L = Σ(sellPx − lotPx)·qtyConsumed. ADJUST(ratio) multiplies
    every open lot qty by ratio, divides its price by ratio — INVARIANT:
    total cost value unchanged to 1e-6 (assert in test).
M8. SIP FV (exists) and STEP-UP SIP: contributions grow g% annually:
    FV = Σ_year a_y · fvFactor(months remaining), a_y = a·(1+g)^y — implement
    by monthly loop (already the pattern in sipArea), don't derive closed form.
M9. GOAL REQUIRED MONTHLY: solve FV formula for the monthly amount at the
    assumed CAGR over remaining months; if targetDate past → show "date
    passed, Sir" state, never divide by zero.

SECTION 5 — NEW ORDERS (1500 SERIES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ORD-1501  VALUE-CHAIN MAPS — "who else wins, later". [P1 · flagship of Part III]
  JDATA.VALUE_CHAINS: per sector, 3 tiers of the supply/beneficiary chain,
  hand-curated once:
    tier1 PRIMES (repriced in minutes — e.g. defence: HAL, BEL),
    tier2 SYSTEMS/COMPONENTS (repriced in days-weeks — forgings, electronics,
          wiring, optics: Bharat Forge, Astra Microwave, Data Patterns...),
    tier3 INPUTS/SERVICES (repriced last — specialty steel, explosives,
          testing, MRO: Solar Inds, Mishra Dhatu...)
  Each entry: {name, role, note}. UI: "WHO ELSE WINS" cascade on pattern and
  idea cards — three glass columns with animated connectors flowing left to
  right (respect FX.enabled), tier badges T1/T2/T3, each name clickable →
  Intel search. Jarvis intent: "second order plays in defence".
  Copy discipline: header carries "for research — reprice lags are
  tendencies, not laws." This encodes E2 into the product.

ORD-1502  CORPORATE ACTIONS TOOL. [P1 — a correctness landmine if skipped]
  Splits/bonuses silently halve prices and create phantom losses in any
  portfolio tool. (a) Manual ADJUST action per holding: dialog "ratio 2:1 /
  1:2 / custom" → creates an ADJUST Txn (M7) with note; ledger shows it.
  (b) DIV transaction type: dividend received (cash in, qty unchanged,
  feeds XIRR). (c) Detection heuristic when quotes are live: price moved
  >40% overnight vs stored cur → amber flag on the row: "possible corporate
  action — verify and use ADJUST", NEVER auto-adjust.

ORD-1503  AMFI NAV SYNC (mutual funds, free, keyless, official). [P1]
  AMFI publishes all MF NAVs as plain text: amfiindia.com/spages/NAVAll.txt
  (Scheme Code;ISIN;ISIN2;Scheme Name;NAV;Date — ~2MB). Relay endpoint
  /amfi (cache 6h, add host to whitelist). Client: holdings of type MF get
  one-time fuzzy match (substring scoring on scheme name) with a
  confirm-picker; store schemeCode on the holding; SYNC PRICES then updates
  MF NAVs alongside Yahoo equities. This makes the Indian MF part of the
  book genuinely accurate — most "net worth apps" get this wrong.

ORD-1504  VERIFICATION TRAIL. [P2]
  Static JDATA.VERIFY map: claimType → where a human verifies it (no URLs
  needed, name the source): order wins → exchange filings (BSE/NSE
  announcements); financials → annual report + screener-type sites;
  promoter pledge → shareholding pattern filings; govt allocations → budget
  documents/PIB; margins guidance → earnings call transcripts.
  Every idea card gets a "VERIFY THIS YOURSELF" expandable listing the 3
  relevant trails for its claim types. Turns ideas from assertions into
  homework assignments — exactly right for G8.

ORD-1505  SUNDAY REVIEW MODE (guided weekly ritual). [P2]
  A 6-step guided flow (glass wizard, ~15 min): 1 new threads since last
  review → 2 thesis board grooming (stale RESEARCHING items nudged) →
  3 expense quick-log for the week → 4 goals delta → 5 one journal prompt
  ("Best decision this week? One thing to stop?" — free text saved) →
  6 backup reminder if last export > 30 days. Gentle streak counter
  (weeks reviewed), no shame on breaks. Entry point: Command Center card
  that appears on weekends + "review" intent.

ORD-1506  PERF HUD + BUDGETS. [P2, dev aid]
  Settings toggle "diagnostics": overlay chip showing FPS (rAF delta EMA),
  active loop count (App._stops length), JS heap (performance.memory when
  available), last render duration per view. BUDGETS to hold: boot→
  interactive < 2.5s; idle CPU with fx on < ~6%; visible canvas loops ≤ 2;
  ingest of 200 items < 150ms; DOM nodes per view < 3000. test.html gains a
  perf case that times Engine.run on a 200-item synthetic corpus.

ORD-1507  STALENESS DOCTRINE (UX rule + implementation). [P1]
  EVERY data element that can age carries its age: quotes ("synced 14:32",
  amber ≥ 15 min, grey/stale ≥ 1 day), news mode chip (SIM / LIVE·HH:MM),
  rollup-derived charts ("as of yesterday"), AMFI NAVs (date from the file).
  One shared helper renders these stamps consistently (U.staleness(ts)).
  A dashboard that admits its age keeps trust when it can't be fresh (PM1,
  PM2).

ORD-1508  BACKUP HYGIENE + ENCRYPTION. [P1 — this repo syncs to OneDrive]
  (a) Add to repo .gitignore: jarvis-backup-*.json (and jarvis/icons/*.png
      if generated locally is fine to commit — icons yes, backups NEVER).
  (b) Export dialog warns: "Contains your full financial ledger. Keep it
      out of the repo and out of synced folders you don't trust."
  (c) Optional passphrase encryption: WebCrypto PBKDF2(150k iters, SHA-256)
      → AES-GCM; file header {v, salt, iv}; import detects and prompts.
      Default ON suggestion when goals/txns exist. Forgotten passphrase =
      unrecoverable file — say so at export time.

ORD-1509  RELAY THREAT-MODEL FIXES. [P0-adjacent, do with ORD-201]
  Bind 127.0.0.1 EXPLICITLY (never 0.0.0.0 — a laptop on hostel/café wifi
  must not serve a proxy to the LAN). Access-Control-Allow-Origin: lock to
  the exact app origin (http://localhost:5500) instead of *. GET only,
  2MB body cap, strip Set-Cookie, whitelist enforced by hostname EXACT
  match (no substring checks — "evil-news.google.com.attacker.io" must fail).
  Log to memory only, never to disk.

ORD-1510  MICROCOPY SYSTEM. [P2, small]
  One pass to standardize: panel labels ALL-CAPS mono ≤ 18 chars; status
  vocabulary fixed set {LIVE, SIM, STALE, SYNCING, OFFLINE, WARMING UP};
  every empty state = what's missing + one action button; every error =
  what failed · what still works · what to do next; negative numbers use
  true minus "−"; tabular-nums on all data columns (mostly done). Add a
  MARKET chip near the clock: OPEN (IST 09:15–15:30 Mon–Fri) / CLOSED /
  WEEKEND — with the honest caveat that exchange holidays aren't tracked
  (tooltip: "holiday calendar not loaded").

ORD-1511  FUTURE-AI SEAM (boundary only — build nothing). [P3]
  The user may allow an LLM "later". Keep exactly three functions isolated
  and rule-based today so a future swap is one file, not a rewrite:
  Summarize(items)→string (ORD-1004), ParseIntent(text)→{intent,slots}
  (Jarvis.handle's matcher), CritiqueThesis(thesis)→string[] (checklist
  nags). Comment each: "seam: replaceable backend". Do NOT add any API
  plumbing now.

ORD-1512  ANNUAL MAINTENANCE PROTOCOL. [P2, mostly docs]
  A dated checklist in ARCHITECTURE.md + a once-a-year gentle reminder
  (first open after Jan 15): refresh JDATA.EVENTS dates (MPC/budget/expiry),
  verify feeds alive (coverage panel makes this visible), re-check Yahoo/
  AMFI endpoints, review USD_INR default, export a yearly backup, and run
  ORD-1513's dictionary review. Keeps the tool from rotting (PM1).

ORD-1513  DICTIONARY EVOLUTION (the system learns vocabulary, human-gated).
  [P2 · elegant loop] Anomaly terms (ORD-1001) that recur across ≥ 3
  separate weeks accumulate in a candidates list (settings → "Lexicon").
  Each shows term, weeks seen, sample headlines, and a "PROMOTE" button →
  user assigns sector/tag → saved to jarvis.userdict.v1, merged into
  JDATA.KEYWORDS at load. The keyword net grows with reality, with a human
  confirming every addition. No AI, no drift.

SECTION 6 — EDGE-CASE INVENTORY (each becomes a test.html assertion)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGINE: empty corpus (all zeros, no NaN) · single item (no clusters, no
  division by zero) · all-bearish corpus (bias 0%, threat board full) ·
  item with 2 amounts takes max · "₹0 crore" ignored · headline ending "?"
  halves verb weight · same story 4 sources → 1 group, 1 flow, impact boost.
THREADS: A~B and B~C but not A~C → one thread (transitive closure), no
  cycles, chapters date-ordered.
PORTFOLIO: SELL > held rejected · ADJUST preserves value to 1e-6 · backdated
  txn inserts in order and recomputes FIFO · two holdings same name
  different symbol stay separate · XIRR "—" under preconditions ·
  demo book load is idempotent (no duplicates on double click).
QUOTES: symbol fails → old price kept + stale amber, never 0 · price −55%
  overnight → corporate-action flag, no auto change · relay down mid-sync →
  partial results applied, rest stale.
EXPENSES: entry at 23:59 vs 00:01 lands in correct months · negative amount
  rejected.
GOALS: past targetDate → "date passed" state · target below current value →
  100% + celebrated once (ORD-1205), not every load.
ALERTS: same rule can't fire twice for the same gid/level-cross · 51st hit
  evicts the oldest.
ARCHIVE: rollup written exactly once per local day even across midnight with
  the tab open (ORD-905) · 91-day-old signals pruned · IDB unavailable
  (private mode) → app fully functional, archive features show "no memory
  available" states.
CHAT: 300-char paste doesn't break layout · restored history doesn't retype
  or re-speak · "clear" wipes store and view atomically.
BACKUP: import of a corrupted/truncated file → clear error, NOTHING
  overwritten (validate fully before applying) · encrypted import with wrong
  passphrase → clear error, no partial state.

SECTION 7 — PERSONA BIBLE (JARVIS's voice is a feature; keep it consistent)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VOICE: British-butler restraint. Competence over enthusiasm. Numbers before
adjectives. Short sentences. Never sycophantic, never scolding. "Sir" or the
configured name. No emoji, no exclamation marks (sole exception: party
protocol). Dry wit rationed to ≤ 1 quip per 6 exchanges, never about losses.

SITUATIONAL LINES (style anchors — write variants in this register):
  gain:      "The book is up 1.8% this week. Adequate. The plan continues."
  loss:      "Down 2.1% this month — entirely the crypto sleeve; the equity
             core is flat. The plan does not require action."
  anomaly:   "The wires are suddenly loud about 'uranium' — nine mentions,
             four sources, no prior baseline. Worth ten minutes, Sir."
  no edge:   "Nothing on the board clears the bar today. The correct trade
             is patience."
  error:     "The uplink failed. The simulation continues; nothing you rely
             on is affected."
  wrong:     "For the record: the March logistics pattern went nowhere —
             it's in the report card. Calibration noted."
  nudge cap: at most ONE unsolicited remark per session (alerts excepted).
SILENCE RULES: JARVIS speaks uninvited only for: the daily first-open brief,
a fired alert, a goal milestone. Never interrupts typing. Never repeats a
declined suggestion (persist declined flags).
TTS: expand "₹27.53 L cr" → "27.53 lakh crore rupees"; strip markup and
bullets before speaking (partially done — verify).

SECTION 8 — THE HUMAN PROTOCOLS (the tool implies a routine; design it)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAILY (1 min): open → auto-brief → at most one click of curiosity. The
  morning screen contains no transactional actions (D1 makes this true).
WEEKLY (15 min): Sunday Review (ORD-1505).
MONTHLY (10 min): read the accountability report card; check rebalance
  drift; export a backup (reminder on the 1st, dismissible).
YEARLY (1 hr): maintenance protocol (ORD-1512) + lexicon review (ORD-1513).
The app supports each ritual but never guilt-trips a missed one. Streaks
inform, never shame.

ASSETS: transactions/goals/expenses (sensitive) · theses/journal (private

EXPOSURES & MITIGATIONS:
  T1 Repo syncs to OneDrive + git remotes → backups must never enter the

  T2 LAN neighbors → relay binds 127.0.0.1, exact-hostname whitelist,

     instructions.
  T4 Shared/stolen laptop → an in-app PIN would be THEATER (localStorage is

     locks; the honest answer is OS user-account separation + encrypted

  T5 Voice input → Chrome STT is cloud-processed; wake word stays opt-in
     with visible armed state (Part II amendment stands).
  T6 Clipboard/exports → "copy brief" excludes personal book numbers by
     default; including them is an explicit checkbox.
  T7 Future-self mistakes → confirm-modals on destructive actions (done),
     import validates fully before applying (Section 6), storage versioning
     with one-way migrations (G9).

SECTION 11 — FINAL WORD: THE HIERARCHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When any two orders conflict, resolve by this ranking:
  1. TRUTH      — numbers that can answer "says who, as of when".
  2. CALM       — no urgency theater, no twitch loops, silence is an output.
  3. DISCIPLINE — process gates over predictions, journals over hunches.
  4. DELIGHT    — the glow, the sweep, the voice. Load-bearing joy, last.
Build a tool the user still trusts — and still enjoys opening — in five
years. That, Sir, is the whole mission.
