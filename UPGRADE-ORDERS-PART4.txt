SECTION 1 — ORIENTATION: THE DEPTH THAT REMAINS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Everything so far detects WHAT is happening (signals, patterns, flows).
The remaining depth is WHY and SO-WHAT:
  · Causal knowledge — event X tends to move sector Y, with a lag and a sign.
  · Epistemic honesty — disagreement, ranges, and self-auditing.
  · The user's own mind — calibration, ownership, and where his real
    leverage lies (hint: at 21, it is not portfolio alpha).
  · Governance — this app will be built by MANY AI sessions over months;
    coherence across sessions is now the biggest engineering risk.
Rule-based systems are only as smart as their encoded knowledge. Part IV is
mostly KNOWLEDGE ENGINEERING — the hard, valuable, human part.

SECTION 2 — ORD-1601 · THE CAUSAL ENGINE  [P1 · flagship of Part IV]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT: a hand-curated causal graph of the economy + a forward-chaining
inference layer. When live signals activate a trigger, JARVIS walks the
graph and shows the implication CHAIN — with sign, lag, mechanism, and
confidence — instead of just clustering headlines.

DATA MODEL (data.js): JDATA.CAUSAL = [{trigger, targets:[{to, sign(+|−|±),
lagQ:[min,max], mech(one line), conf(L|M|H)}]}]. Triggers map to existing
tags/keywords (Central Banks, Trade Policy, Capex, crude, monsoon...).

INFERENCE RULES:
  · A trigger is ACTIVE when ≥ 2 corroborated signals matched it in 72h.
  · Max 2 hops. Chain confidence = min of edge confidences, downgraded one
    level on the second hop. Show hops explicitly, never just the endpoint.
  · Opposing active edges on the same target are BOTH shown, labelled
    "CROSS-CURRENTS" — never silently netted (see ORD-1602).
  · Every chain renders as: [TRIGGER] ──(lag 1-2q · mechanism)──▶ [TARGET ±]
    A CSS/HTML chain component with the existing chip/glass language;
    animated connector sweep gated by FX.enabled.

SURFACES: "IMPLICATIONS" section on pattern cards; a CAUSAL CASCADE panel on
Command Center when ≥ 1 trigger is active; Jarvis intents: "what does the
rate cut mean", "trace crude", "implications for my book" (intersect chain
targets with the user's holdings' sectors → per-holding ± chips, phrased as
tendencies).

SEED GRAPH (~28 edges; Sonnet transcribes as data, then the human extends):
  rate cut        → banks ± (NIM squeeze 0-1q −ve; credit growth 1-2q +ve) M
                  → autos/realty + (cheaper EMIs, 1-2q) H
                  → INR − (carry unwind, 0-1q) M → IT/pharma + (export FX) M
  rate hike       → inverse of the above M
  crude ↑         → paints/aviation/tyres/OMCs − (input costs, 0-1q) H
                  → CPI ↑ (0-1q) H → rate-cut odds − (0-1q) H
  crude ↓         → inverse H
  INR weakness    → IT/pharma exporters + (0-1q) H; importers/aviation − H
  China stimulus  → metals + (0-2q) H; global risk-on + (0-1q) M
  Taiwan/China+1  → India electronics/chemicals mfg + (2-8q, relocation) M
  US Fed cut      → FII flows into EM + (0-1q) H → Indian largecaps + M
  import tariff (e.g. steel safeguard)
                  → domestic producers + (0-1q) H; user industries (autos,
                    capex) − (margin squeeze, 0-2q) M
  budget capex ↑  → infra/rail/defence ORDER BOOKS + (1-4q) H
                  → execution REVENUE + (4-12q — the market often front-runs
                    hop 1 and underprices hop 2) M
  PLI scheme      → target-sector capacity/capex + (4-12q) H
  good monsoon    → rural demand + → FMCG/tractors/2-wheelers + (1-2q) M
  weak monsoon    → food CPI ↑ (0-1q) H → rate cuts delayed M
  gold ↑ persistent → risk-off regime evidence (input to ORD-1610) M
  defence export deal → sector re-rating + (0-1q) M
  data-center buildout → power demand + → gencos/transmission/cables +
                    (2-8q) H
  EV price war    → OEM margins − (0-2q) H; component volume players ±
                    (cost-plus insulated) M
  credit growth ↑ → private capex confirmation → industrials + (1-4q) M
  IPO flood       → secondary-market liquidity drain − (0-1q) L
  FII sell + DII SIP buy → largecap range-bound; flows tug-of-war (regime
                    evidence) M
  critical-mineral export curbs → EV supply chain − (0-2q) M; domestic
                    mining/recycling + (4-12q) L
ACCEPTANCE: chains render with all 4 elements (sign, lag, mechanism,
confidence); "implications for my book" answers within 1s; every chain's
wording says "tends to", never "will".

SECTION 3 — EPISTEMIC UPGRADES (1602–1605)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ORD-1602  CONTRADICTION LEDGER. [P1, cheap, high trust]
  When corroboration grouping finds signals on the same entity/sector with
  OPPOSITE sentiment within 72h, do not average them into "neutral".
  Create a Dissent record {topic, bullSignals[], bearSignals[]} and render a
  small "CROSS-CURRENTS" list on the Patterns view: "Defence: 3 bullish
  (orders) vs 2 bearish (valuation warnings)". Averaging disagreement into
  mush is how dashboards lie; displayed dissent is how they earn trust.
  Jarvis: "where is the disagreement?" lists them.

ORD-1603  SOURCE DIVERSITY INDEX + SYNDICATION DETECTOR. [P2]
  Corroboration is fake if all "5 sources" republish one wire story.
  Detect syndication: body-shingle Jaccard ≥ 0.8 across different source
  names → count as ONE effective source (keep names, mark "syndicated").
  Coverage panel (ORD-904) adds a diversity line: effective sources /
  nominal sources + a Herfindahl-style concentration note when one outlet
  exceeds 50% of the day's signals ("your view of the world is mostly
  one newsroom today, Sir").

ORD-1604  CONSERVATION LAWS (production self-audit). [P1, small]
  Invariants checked on every data:updated (throw → single toast + console
  detail, never silent): dedupedTotal ≤ rawTotal; every Flow traces to a
  Group; every Idea's conviction equals its components' weighted sum ±0.5;
  rollup dates unique; no Signal has senti outside {bull,bear,neut};
  FIFO book value equals Σ lots to 1e-6. Surface a tiny "SELF-AUDIT OK"
  line in the settings diagnostics block with last-checked time. The
  dashboard that audits itself is the one future-you believes.

ORD-1605  SCENARIO BANDS EVERYWHERE. [P1, small]
  Single-point projections are the most common personal-finance lie.
  Every projection surface (SIP projector, goals, step-up, FIRE) renders
  THREE lines/values: conservative 8% · base 12% · optimistic 15% (rates
  configurable in settings). The chart shows a shaded band, the headline
  shows the base with the range in smaller mono ("₹75.7 L · range 58–94").
  Jarvis quotes ranges by default. Goal on-track logic uses the
  CONSERVATIVE band (under-promise by design).

SECTION 4 — THE USER IS PART OF THE SYSTEM (1606–1609)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ORD-1606  PREDICTION BOOK + CALIBRATION COACH. [P1 · the deepest personal
  feature in this program]
  Record explicit predictions: {statement, direction, subject(sector/
  symbol/macro), horizonDate, confidence 50-95%, createdAt, resolvedAt?,
  outcome? (hit|miss|push), resolveHint?(numeric level when applicable)}.
  Entry via Jarvis ("predict: defence outperforms nifty by year end, 70%")
  or a small form in Ideas Lab. Resolution: manual prompt when horizonDate
  arrives (+ auto-suggest via proxy quotes when numeric).
  CALIBRATION VIEW (in Ideas Lab or report card): bucket predictions by
  stated confidence (50-60, 60-70...), plot stated vs actual hit-rate —
  the classic calibration curve — plus Brier score = mean((conf −
  outcome)²). JARVIS's quarterly line writes itself: "Your 80%-confidence
  calls land 55% of the time — you are overconfident by 25 points. Your
  60% calls are well calibrated. Adjust position sizes accordingly."
  Nothing in this entire program will make the user more money than
  learning his own calibration. Pure arithmetic. Needs ≥ 10 resolved
  predictions before the curve renders (show progress until then).

ORD-1607  OWNERSHIP GATE (amends ORD-1102 checklist). [P1, tiny]
  Add a mandatory checklist field: "Thesis restated in YOUR OWN words"
  (textarea, ≥ 2 sentences, cannot paste-match the generated thesis —
  reject ≥ 60% shingle overlap with the machine text). The tool must never
  let the user outsource conviction to it; if he cannot write why he owns
  something, he does not own it — the position owns him. This is the
  single most important guardrail in the app.

ORD-1608  ALPHA AUDIT — where the user's leverage actually is. [P1]
  Compute annually-denominated impact from HIS real data:
    a) +1pp portfolio return on current book value
    b) +10% income (career growth) at current savings behavior
    c) −5% expenses redirected to SIP
    d) +5pp savings rate
  Render ranked bars (My Money panel) + one Jarvis verdict. For a student
  book (~₹9L) vs a ₹90k/mo income, career growth beats return-chasing by
  an order of magnitude — SAY SO: "Your career compounds ~12× harder than
  your portfolio right now, Sir. The terminal is for the surplus; the
  library is for the alpha." Include the crossover estimate: the year the
  book (at conservative growth) starts to out-earn a 10% raise. Reframes
  "make me money faster" toward the truth without lecturing.

ORD-1609  RE-ENTRY BRIEF. [P2]
  If lastOpen > 21 days ago, suppress the daily brief and render a
  compressed WELCOME-BACK brief instead: days away · what compounded
  quietly (SIP months banked, book delta) · which threads resolved or died
  · current regime vs when he left · ONE suggested action (usually: log
  anything that happened offline). Never a backlog dump, never guilt.
  Returning users are lost at re-entry; design for the comeback.

SECTION 5 — ORD-1610 · REGIME AWARENESS [P2]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A crude-but-honest regime classifier from data already on hand:
  Inputs: bias% trend (7d), FII flow direction (if live), gold signal
  frequency, threat-board size, index trend (if quotes live).
  Output: one of RISK-ON / RISK-OFF / ROTATION / NO-READ (default when
  inputs conflict — NO-READ is a first-class answer, not a failure).
Topbar chip next to MARKET status + one line in the brief ("Regime: risk-on,
11th day"). Playbook emphasis shifts wording only (risk-off → briefs lead
with threat board and cash notes; risk-on → leads with patterns). Label the
whole thing honestly in its tooltip: "heuristic, 4 inputs, no magic."
Static seasonality notes join JDATA.EVENTS (budget season, results seasons,
monsoon window) as context lines, not signals.

SECTION 7 — GOVERNANCE ACROSS MANY AI SESSIONS (1613–1615)
The real engineering risk from here: DRIFT. Dozens of future model sessions,
each locally sensible, collectively incoherent. Constitution + decision log
+ pruning are the immune system.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ORD-1613  THE CONSTITUTION. [P0 for process — create immediately]
  Create jarvis/CONSTITUTION.md containing exactly these articles (copy
  verbatim, then keep it under 60 lines forever):
    Art. 1  Truth > Calm > Discipline > Delight. Ties break upward.
    Art. 2  No buy buttons. The app records decisions; it never executes,
            urges, or counts down.
    Art. 3  Silence is a valid output. Nothing auto-refreshes; no urgency
            theater; at most one unsolicited nudge per session.
    Art. 4  Every number must answer "says who, as of when" (source +
            staleness stamps).
    Art. 5  Disagreement is displayed, never averaged away.
    Art. 6  Every conclusion must be explainable as a one-line chain a
            human can read (no opaque scores without breakdowns).
    Art. 7  Data never leaves this machine except whitelisted public GETs.
            No accounts, no telemetry, no cloud.
    Art. 8  The sidebar holds six views, forever. New features nest inside.
    Art. 9  Projections show ranges, never single points.
    Art.10  Delete before add: any feature unused for 60 days is a removal
            candidate (ORD-1615).
    Art.11  The user's own words gate every commitment (ORD-1607).
    Art.12  Only the human amends this constitution. Models may propose.
  EVERY future session preamble (give the user this snippet to paste):
    "Read jarvis/CONSTITUTION.md and jarvis/ARCHITECTURE.md first. Implement
     ORD-<n> from the UPGRADE-ORDERS files. Minimal diffs; follow the Part
     III data contracts; run test.html and the Part I §11 smoke test; append
     to DECISIONS.md; do not violate the constitution."

ORD-1614  DECISIONS.md (ADR-lite). [P1, tiny]
  Append-only log; one entry per meaningful choice: date · session · what ·
  why · alternatives rejected. Ten lines max each. Future sessions read it
  before re-litigating old choices. Seed it retroactively with: no-build
  vanilla stack; validated 6-color data palette; keyword-net + archive
  design; localStorage/IDB split; no-AI constraint; relay whitelist.

ORD-1615  LOCAL USAGE COUNTERS + PRUNING PROTOCOL. [P2]
  Count view visits, panel expands, intent uses (jarvis.usage.v1 — local
  only, never exported by default). Quarterly, settings shows a "dust
  report": features untouched in 60+ days with a REMOVE suggestion. The
  roadmap includes a chainsaw; a personal tool stays alive by staying small.
  (Counters themselves respect Art. 7 — they never leave the machine.)

SECTION 9 — ACADEMIC FRAMING (this is still a 6th-sem project; win the viva)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Position JARVIS academically as a KNOWLEDGE-BASED EXPERT SYSTEM — textbook
AI, no LLM required, and the absence of an LLM becomes a FEATURE:
  · Knowledge base: JDATA dictionaries, playbooks, value chains, CAUSAL
    graph (ORD-1601).
  · Inference engine: forward chaining over causal rules with confidence
    propagation and lag reasoning.
  · Explanation facility: every conclusion shows its chain (Art. 6) — the
    classic expert-system property modern LLMs LACK. Lead with this.
  · Knowledge acquisition: human-gated dictionary evolution (ORD-1513).
  · Plus: statistical anomaly detection (z-scores = classic IR/statistics),
    TF-style term analytics, calibration scoring (Brier) = decision theory.
CS concepts to name-drop honestly in the report: information retrieval
(shingling, Jaccard, dedup), expert systems (rules + explanation), HCI
(a11y, reduced-motion, progressive disclosure), local-first architecture
(IndexedDB, PWA, offline), speech interfaces (Web Speech), security (CSP,
threat model, whitelisted proxy), software engineering (golden-file tests,
invariants, ADRs, constitution-driven multi-agent development — that last
one is genuinely novel material for a report).
VIVA Q&A PREP (put in the docs pack, ORD-1402):
  "Where is the ML?" → rule-based + statistical by DESIGN for explainability
    and privacy; show the anomaly z-score and calibration math.
  "Where is the database?" → IndexedDB + versioned localStorage; local-first
    is an architectural position (privacy, zero-cost, offline), not a gap.
  "Why no framework?" → zero dependencies to rot, sub-second loads, and the
    5-year-survival goal; show the event bus as evidence of structure.
  "What is novel?" → explainable causal chains over live news + calibration
    coaching + constitution-governed multi-session AI development.
  "Scalability?" → single-user by explicit design; discuss what WOULD change
    (relay → shared service, IDB → server DB) to show you know.

  CLASSIC BUYER MISTAKES: buying the cheapest P/B without asking why ·
    ignoring deposit franchise quality · extrapolating credit booms ·

SECTION 11 — THE STOPPING RULE (when the correct order is: stop building)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This program does not end when the backlog is empty — backlogs never empty.
It ends when ALL of the following hold:
  1. The morning protocol takes < 60 seconds and you trust every number on
     the screen (Part III §K).
  2. The last two features you added did not change any decision you made.
  3. Maintenance is one hour a year (ORD-1512) and nothing feels broken.
  4. The archive is aging and the calibration curve has data.
At that point the highest-return move available is not ORD-anything. It is:
live your life, log your decisions, resolve your predictions, and let the
archive and the calibration curve age like wine. The tool's final purpose
was never to be built — it was to make its owner a calmer, better-calibrated
investor. When that's true, JARVIS is finished, even if the backlog isn't.
